## Results

- **Setting (linear partial monitoring, Section 2).** "Let $\mathcal A$ be the action space and
  $\mathcal L$ the loss space, which are subsets of $\mathbb R^d$ with $\mathcal A$ compact." "The signal
  function is a known function $\Phi:\mathcal A\times\mathcal L\to\Sigma$ for some observation space
  $\Sigma$." "An adversary and learner interact over $n$ rounds." "First the adversary secretly chooses
  $(\ell_t)_{t=1}^n$ with $\ell_t\in\mathcal L$ for all $t$"; then the learner samples an action
  $A_t\in\mathcal A$ from a distribution depending on past observations $\Phi(A_s,\ell_s)$. Regret:
  $$\mathfrak R_n(\pi,(\ell_t)_{t=1}^n)=\max_{a\in\mathcal A}\mathbb E\Big[\sum_{t=1}^n\langle A_t-a,\ell_t\rangle\Big],$$
  "where the expectation is with respect to the randomness in the actions." Minimax regret:
  $$\mathfrak R_n^*=\inf_\pi\sup_{(\ell_t)_{t=1}^n}\mathfrak R_n(\pi,(\ell_t)_{t=1}^n),$$
  "where the infimum is over all policies and the supremum over all loss sequences in
  $\mathcal L^n$." The $k$-armed bandit: "The standard $k$-armed bandit is recovered when
  $\mathcal A=\{e_1,\dots,e_k\}$, $\mathcal L=[0,1]^k$", with $\Phi(a,\ell)=\langle a,\ell\rangle\in\Sigma=[0,1]$.
- **Lemma 10 (summary).** A shifted importance-weighted loss estimator for OSMD with the
  $1/2$-Tsallis potential, with "$\mathrm{stab}(\mathscr A;\eta)\le k^{1/2}/2+12k\eta$" for "any
  $\eta\le1/2$". Statement not copied in full.
- **Theorem 11.** "The regret of OSMD with the loss estimator of Lemma 10 and appropriate learning
  rate satisfies: $\mathfrak R_n\le\sqrt{2kn}+48k$."
  Context: "The best known bound on the minimax regret for $k$-armed bandits is
  $\mathfrak R_n\le\sqrt{2kn}$ by [21]" ([21] is LS19); the introduction calls the result "an
  efficient algorithm for adversarial $k$-armed bandits with regret $\mathfrak R_n\le\sqrt{2kn}+O(k)$,
  matching the information-theoretic upper bound except for small lower-order terms." The learning
  rate is tuned with the horizon (Corollary 6).
- **Stochastic vs adversarial.** No sentence found stating that adversarial regret bounds imply
  stochastic ones. Section 2: "Any instance of OSMD with a uniform bound on the adversarial regret
  enjoys the same bound on the Bayesian regret" (for any prior).

## Techniques

Online stochastic mirror descent (Algorithm 1) analysed through a stability coefficient; shown to
bound the information ratio of a modified Thompson sampling (Theorem 4), which links the
information-theoretic analysis of LS19 to OSMD. For $k$-armed bandits, the $1/2$-Tsallis potential
$F(x)=-2\sum_{i=1}^k\sqrt{x_i}$ with shifted importance-weighted estimators (Lemma 10), which reduce
the worst-case variance and turn the $\sqrt{8nk}$ bound of the naive estimator into Theorem 11.
