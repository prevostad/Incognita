## Results

Summaries in our words, with short quotations; formulas in the thesis's symbols.

- **§1.4.** Chapter 3 is based on AJK21 (COLT 2021); Chapter 6 on Agrawal, Koolen and Juneja, *Optimal
  Best-Arm Identification Methods for Tail-Risk Measures* (NeurIPS 2021, key AKJ21).
- **§3.1.** Among related work, Lattimore (2017) "considers distributions with a known uniform bound on
  the kurtosis"; none of the algorithms cited there exactly matches the first-order lower bound. The
  thesis contains no other occurrence of "kurtosis" (besides the bibliography).
- **§6.1 and §6.3.2 (CVaR).** The lower KL projection under a CVaR constraint is non-convex. Using the
  variational (minimisation) formula of CVaR, it is rewritten as a minimisation over two variables,
  "fixing one of which resulted in convex optimization over the other". **Theorem 6.5(b):**
  $\mathrm{KL}^L_{\inf}(\eta,v)=\min_{x_0\in[\cdot,v]}\max_{\gamma\in R_2(x_0,v)}\mathbb E_\eta\log g^L(X,\gamma,v,x_0)$, an outer minimisation over a
  scalar $x_0$ in a **compact interval** (from a range lemma, Lemma 6.1) of a concave dual problem. After
  Proposition 6.6: the outer minimisation in $x_0$ "is not even quasi-convex"; the experiments use a
  heuristic bisection.
- **Appendix 6.D.1, (6.55).** For the concentration of $N_i\,\mathrm{KL}^L_{\inf}(\hat\eta_i,c_p(\eta_i))$, the outer variable is fixed
  at the true value-at-risk $x_p(\eta_i)$, which bounds the statistic by a maximum over the dual variables;
  the mixture of supermartingales over a uniform prior on the dual region then applies (Lemma 6.44 =
  Lemma 3.6, the universal-portfolio bound).
- **§6.10 (Discussion and Future Directions).** The authors "believe that the KLinf-based technique can be
  extended" to the class $\tilde{\mathcal L}=\{\kappa\in\mathcal P(\mathbb R):\mathbb E_\kappa|X-m(\kappa)|^{1+\epsilon}\le B\}$ of laws with a bounded centred
  moment, which is non-convex (an example with two Dirac masses); for the mean objective,
  $\mathrm{KL}^U_{\inf,\tilde{\mathcal L}}(\eta,x)=\min\{\mathrm{KL}(\eta,\kappa):\kappa\in\tilde{\mathcal L},m(\kappa)\ge x\}$ can be rewritten as
  $\min_{y\ge x}\min\{\mathrm{KL}(\eta,\kappa):\mathbb E_\kappa(X)=y,\ \mathbb E_\kappa|X-y|^{1+\epsilon}\le B\}$; "a concentration result
  similar" to those of the chapter "can be proven" for this problem, but "some work is needed to
  establish the continuity of these new projection functions and identify the extreme distributions for
  this new class", left as "a topic for separate research".

## Techniques

- Convexification of a non-convex $\mathrm{KL}_{\inf}$ by an outer minimisation over a scalar parameter (here the
  location of the variational formula of CVaR, or the mean for centred moments), with a convex inner
  problem treated by Lagrangian duality (§6.3.2, §6.10).
- Upper deviation with the outer parameter fixed at its true value, then a mixture of supermartingales
  over the dual parameters (App. 6.D.1).
