## Results

Statements copied from the page images of the PMLR PDF. $\Sigma_K$ is the simplex of proportions on
the $K$ arms, $\mathcal A$ the set of arms, $N_a(t)$ the number of draws of arm $a$ up to time $t$,
$w^*(\boldsymbol\mu)$ the optimal proportions, $\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}(t)$ the maximum likelihood estimate.

- **C-Tracking (§3.1, p. 7).** "for every $\epsilon\in(0,1/K]$, let $w^\epsilon(\boldsymbol\mu)$ be a $L^\infty$
  projection of $w^*(\boldsymbol\mu)$ onto $\Sigma_K^\epsilon=\{(w_1,\dots,w_K)\in[\epsilon,1]:w_1+\dots+w_K=1\}$. Choosing
  $\epsilon_t=(K^2+t)^{-1/2}/2$ and $A_{t+1}\in\operatorname{argmax}_{1\le a\le K}\sum_{s=0}^tw_a^{\epsilon_s}(\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}(s))-N_a(t)$, we prove in
  Appendix B that:"
- **Lemma 7 (p. 7).** "For all $t\ge1$ and $a\in\mathcal A$, the C-Tracking rule ensures that
  $N_a(t)\ge\sqrt{t+K^2}-2K$ and that $\max_{1\le a\le K}\Big|N_a(t)-\sum_{s=0}^{t-1}w_a^*(\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}(s))\Big|\le K(1+\sqrt t)$."
- **Lemma 8 (p. 7, D-Tracking).** "The D-Tracking rule ensures that $N_a(t)\ge(\sqrt t-K/2)_+-1$ and that
  for all $\epsilon>0$, for all $t_0$, there exists $t_\epsilon\ge t_0$ such that
  $\sup_{t\ge t_0}\max_a|w_a^*(\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}(t))-w_a^*(\boldsymbol\mu)|\le\epsilon\Rightarrow\sup_{t\ge t_\epsilon}\max_a\Big|\frac{N_a(t)}t-w_a^*(\boldsymbol\mu)\Big|\le3(K-1)\epsilon$."
- **Lemma 15 (Appendix B.1, "Tracking a cumulated sum of proportions", p. 20).** "Let $K$ be a positive
  integer, let $\Sigma_K$ be the simplex of dimension $K-1$, and for every $i\in\{1,\dots,K\}$, let $\delta_i$ be
  the vertex of $\Sigma_K$ with a 1 on coordinate $i$. For a positive integer $n$, let
  $p(1),p(2),\dots,p(n)\in\Sigma_K$ and for every $k\le n$ let $P(k)=p(1)+\dots+p(k)$. Define $N(0)=0$, for every
  $k\in\{0,\dots,n-1\}$ $I_{k+1}\in\operatorname{argmax}_{1\le i\le K}[P_i(k+1)-N_i(k)]$ and $N(k+1)=N(k)+\delta_{I_{k+1}}$. Then
  $\max_{1\le i\le K}|N_i(n)-P_i(n)|\le K-1$."
- **(12) (p. 20).** Lemma 15 with $p(k)=w^{\epsilon_{k-1}}(\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}(k-1))$ gives
  $\max_{1\le a\le K}\Big|N_a(t)-\sum_{s=0}^{t-1}w_a^{\epsilon_s}(\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}(s))\Big|\le K-1$, from which Lemma 7 follows with
  $\sum_s\epsilon_s$ compared with an integral.
- **Remark 16 (p. 21).** The bound is "probably overly pessimistic", but no bound independent of $K$ is
  possible: with $p_i(k)=\mathbb 1\{i\ge k\}/(K-k+1)$ and the smallest index chosen in the argmax, one gets
  $N_K(K-1)=0$ and $P_K(K-1)=1/K+1/(K-1)+\dots+1/2\sim\log(K)$.

## Techniques

- Proof of Lemma 15 (p. 21): first $\max_iN_i(k)-P_i(k)\le1$ by induction on $k$ (the pulled arm is the
  one with the largest, hence positive, deficit, since the deficits sum to $0$); then the total positive
  deficit $r_k=\sum_{i=1}^K(P_i(k)-N_i(k))_+$ satisfies $r_k\le K-1$, by induction on $k$, distinguishing
  whether the pulled arm is still in deficit after its pull.
- Forced exploration by projection of the target proportions onto $\Sigma_K^\epsilon$ (C-Tracking), or by
  pulling an under-sampled arm (D-Tracking), §3.1.
