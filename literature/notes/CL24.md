## Setting (§2, §2.1)

- $K$ arms $\mathcal A=\{1,\dots,K\}$. Warm-start phase: for each $a$ the DM receives $T_{\mathrm S}(a)\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$
  i.i.d. offline samples $X_1(a),\dots,X_{T_{\mathrm S}(a)}(a)\sim P^{(\mathrm{off})}_a$. Online phase of $T$ steps with
  rewards $R(a)\sim P^{(\mathrm{on})}_a$. The sizes $T_{\mathrm S}(a)$ are part of the instance
  $I=(\mathcal A,\{T_{\mathrm S}(a)\}_{a\in\mathcal A},P,T)$, $P=(P^{(\mathrm{off})},P^{(\mathrm{on})})$: they are fixed (not
  chosen by the policy), and the policy sees $S$ before the online phase. "The DM only knows $\mathcal A$ and
  $S$, but not $P$ and $T$, at the start of the online phase."
- "For every $a\in\mathcal A$, we assume that both $P^{(\mathrm{off})}_a,P^{(\mathrm{on})}_a$ are 1-subGaussian, which is
  also known to the DM." No boundedness assumption on the means. Randomised non-anticipatory policies
  are allowed in the lower bounds.
- Regret (3): $\mathrm{Reg}_T(\pi,P)=T\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}_*-\sum_{t=1}^T\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(A_t)=\sum_{t=1}^T\Delta(A_t)$, with
  $\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}_*=\max_{a\in\mathcal A}\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)$, $\Delta(a)=\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}_*-\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)$.
- **Valid bias bound, §2.1, (4).** "We say that $V=\{V(a)\}_{a\in K}\in(\mathbb R_{\geq0}\cup\{\infty\})^{|\mathcal A|}$ is a
  valid *bias bound* on an instance $I$ if
  $V(a)\geq|\mu^{(\mathrm{off})}(a)-\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)|\quad\text{for each }a\in\mathcal A.$ (4)"

## Results

### Algorithm 1, Policy MIN-UCB (§4, p. 13), copied

> 1: **Input:** Valid bias bound $V$ on the instance, confidence parameter $\{\delta_t\}_{t=1}^\infty$, offline samples $S$.
> 2: For each $a\in\mathcal A$, compute $\hat X(a)=\frac{\sum_{s=1}^{T_{\mathrm S}(a)}X_s(a)}{T_{\mathrm S}(a)}$, and initialize $\hat R_1(a)=0$.
> 3: At $t=1,\dots,K$, pull each arm once, then set $N_{K+1}(a)=1$ for all $a$.
> 4: **for** $t=K+1,\dots,T$ **do**
> 5: For all $a\in\mathcal A$, compute the vanilla UCB
> $$\mathrm{UCB}_t(a)=\hat R_t(a)+\mathrm{rad}_t(a),\qquad(7)$$
> where $\mathrm{rad}_t(a)=\sqrt{\frac{2\log(2t/\delta_t)}{N_t(a)}}$.
> 6: For all $a\in\mathcal A$, compute the warm-start UCB: $\mathrm{UCB}^{\mathrm S}_t(a)=$
> $$\frac{N_t(a)\cdot\hat R_t(a)+T_{\mathrm S}(a)\cdot\hat X(a)}{N_t(a)+T_{\mathrm S}(a)}+\mathrm{rad}^{\mathrm S}_t(a),\qquad(8)$$
> where $\mathrm{rad}^{\mathrm S}_t(a)=$
> $$\sqrt{\frac{2\log(2t/\delta_t)}{N_t(a)+T_{\mathrm S}(a)}}+\frac{T_{\mathrm S}(a)}{N_t(a)+T_{\mathrm S}(a)}\cdot V(a).\qquad(9)$$
> 7: Select $A_t\in\operatorname{argmax}_{a\in\mathcal A}\{\min\{\mathrm{UCB}_t(a),\mathrm{UCB}^{\mathrm S}_t(a)\}\}$.
> 8: Observe $R_t\sim P^{(\mathrm{on})}_{A_t}$. For each $a\in\mathcal A$, update $N_{t+1}(a)=N_t(a)+\mathbf 1(A_t=a)$,
> $$\hat R_{t+1}(a)=\begin{cases}\frac{N_t(a)\cdot\hat R_t(a)}{N_t(a)+1}+\frac{R_t}{N_t(a)+1}&a=A_t\\ \hat R_t(a)&a\neq A_t\end{cases}.$$

What it needs: $V$ (input), $\{\delta_t\}$ (input; Theorem 2 takes $\delta_t=1/(2Kt^2)$, so $K$), the offline
samples (hence $T_{\mathrm S}(a)$ and $\hat X(a)$); the sub-Gaussian constant 1 is built into the factor 2
of the radii; $T$ is not used. As printed, step 3 sets $N_{K+1}(a)=1$ but does not say that $\hat R_{K+1}(a)$
is the reward of the initial pull, and $\hat X(a)$ is $0/0$ when $T_{\mathrm S}(a)=0$ (then (8)–(9) reduce to (7)
if $T_{\mathrm S}(a)\hat X(a)$ is read as 0). Text after Algorithm 1: with no knowledge, "it is advisable to set
$V(a)=\infty$ for all $a$, which specializes MIN-UCB to the vanilla UCB."

### Lemma 1 (§4, p. 14)

With $\xi_t=\cap_{a\in\mathcal A}(\xi_t(a)\cap\xi^{\mathrm S}_t(a))$, $\xi_t(a)=\{\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)\le\mathrm{UCB}_t(a)\le\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)+2\mathrm{rad}_t(a)\}$
and $\xi^{\mathrm S}_t(a)=\{\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)\le\mathrm{UCB}^{\mathrm S}_t(a)\le\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)+\mathrm{rad}^{\mathrm S}_t(a)+\sqrt{\frac{2\log(2t/\delta_t)}{N_t(a)+T_{\mathrm S}(a)}}+\frac{T_{\mathrm S}(a)\cdot(\mu^{(\mathrm{off})}(a)-\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a))}{N_t(a)+T_{\mathrm S}(a)}\}$:
"**Lemma 1.** $\Pr(\xi_t)\geq1-2K\delta_t$." Proof (App. B.1) not read.

### §4.1, copied

- (10): $\omega(a)=V(a)+(\mu^{(\mathrm{off})}(a)-\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a))$; "By the validity of $V$, we know that $\omega(a)\in[0,2V(a)]$."
- **Theorem 2.** "Consider policy MIN-UCB, which inputs a valid bias bound $V$ on the underlying instance
  $I$ and $\delta_t=1/(2Kt^2)$ for $t=1,2,\ldots$. We have $\mathbb E[\mathrm{Reg}_T(\textit{MIN-UCB},P)]\leq$
  $$O\left(\sum_{a:\Delta(a)>0}\max\left\{\frac{\log(T)}{\Delta(a)}-\mathit{Sav}_0(a),\Delta(a)\right\}\right),\qquad(11)$$
  where $\sum_{a:\Delta(a)>0}$ is over $\{a\in\mathcal A:\Delta(a)>0\}$, and
  $$\mathit{Sav}_0(a)=T_{\mathrm S}(a)\cdot\Delta(a)\cdot\max\left\{1-\frac{\omega(a)}{\Delta(a)},0\right\}^2.\qquad(12)$$"
  Proof in App. B.2 (not read); "A full explicit bound is in (32) in the Appendix" (not read).
- **Definition 1.** "Let $V=(V(a))_{a\in\mathcal A}\in\mathbb R^K_{\geq0}$. We define
  $\mathcal I_V=\{I:|\mu^{(on)}(a)-\mu^{(off)}(a)|\leq V(a)\text{ for all }a\in\mathcal A\}$."
- **Corollary 1.** "For any $V\in\mathbb R^K_{\geq0}$, the MIN-UCB policy with input $V$ and $\delta_t=1/(2Kt^2)$
  satisfies $\mathbb E[\mathrm{Reg}_T(\textit{MIN-UCB},P)]\leq$ (11), for all $I\in\mathcal I_V$."
- **Definition 2.** "An instance $I$ is a Gaussian instance if $P^{(on)}_a,P^{(off)}_a$ are Gaussian distributions
  with variance one, for every $a\in\mathcal A$."
- **Definition 3.** "For $C>0,p\in(0,1)$ and a collection $\mathcal I$ of instances, a non-anticipatory policy $\pi$
  is said to be $(C,p)$-consistent on $\mathcal I$, if for all $I\in\mathcal I$, it holds that
  $\mathbb E[\mathrm{Reg}_T(P,\pi)]\leq C\cdot T^p$." (The argument order $(P,\pi)$ is as printed; elsewhere
  $(\pi,P)$. The quantifier over $T$ is not written; the proof uses the bound at the given $T$.)
  Then: "For example, the vanilla UCB is $(C,1/2)$ consistent for some $C$ proportional to $\sqrt K$."
- **Theorem 3.** "Let $V\in\mathbb R^K_{\geq0}$ be arbitrary, and consider a fixed but arbitrary Gaussian
  instance $I\in\mathcal I_V$. For any $(C,p)$-consistent policy $\pi$ on $\mathcal I_V$, we have the following
  regret lower bound of $\pi$ on $I$: $\mathbb E[\mathrm{Reg}_T(\pi,P)]\geq$
  $$\sum_{a:\Delta(a)>0}\left\{\frac{2(1-p)}{(1+\epsilon)^2}\cdot\frac{\log T}{\Delta(a)}-\mathit{Sav}_\epsilon(a)+\kappa_\epsilon(a)\right\}\qquad(13)$$
  holds for any $\epsilon\in(0,1]$, where $\mathit{Sav}_\epsilon(a)=$
  $$T_{\mathrm S}(a)\cdot\Delta(a)\cdot\max\left\{1-\frac{\omega(a)}{(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)},0\right\}^2,\qquad(14)$$
  and $\kappa_\epsilon(a)=\frac{1}{2(1+\epsilon)^2\Delta(a)}\log\frac{\epsilon\Delta(a)}{8C}$ is independent of $T$ and $T_{\mathrm S}(a)$."
  After it: proved in App. D.2; "Modulo the additive term $\kappa_\epsilon(a)$, the regret upper bound (11)
  and lower bound (13) are nearly matching"; offline data on $a$ help "if and only if
  $\omega(a)<(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)$"; when $P^{(\mathrm{off})}=P^{(\mathrm{on})}$ the bound reduces to
  $\Omega(\sum_a[\frac{\log T}{\Delta(a)}-T_{\mathrm S}(a)\Delta(a)+\kappa_\epsilon(a)])$, claimed novel.
- **Lemma 2 (§4.2).** "Let $a$ be a sub-optimal arm, that is $\Delta(a)>0$. Conditioned on the event $\xi_t$, if it
  holds that $N_t(a)>32\cdot\frac{\log(4Kt^4)}{\Delta(a)^2}-T_{\mathrm S}(a)\cdot\max\left\{1-\frac{\omega(a)}{\Delta(a)},0\right\}^2$,
  then $A_t\neq a$ with certainty." Proof not read beyond its first line.
- **Theorem 1 (§3), impossibility without $V$** (read by the P002 agent; restated here from p. 11): two
  arms, $Q^{(\mathrm{off})}=P^{(\mathrm{off})}$, a policy with $\mathbb E[\mathrm{Reg}_T(\pi,P)]\le CT^{\beta-\epsilon}\log T$ on $I_P$
  suffers $\Omega(T^{1-\beta})$ on $I_Q$ (5).

### Appendix D.1 (pp. 53–55)

- **Claim.** "For $P_i=\mathcal N(\mu_i,\sigma^2)$, where $i\in\{1,2\}$, we have $KL(P_1,P_2)=\frac{(\mu_1-\mu_2)^2}{2\sigma^2}$."
- **Theorem 10** (Bretagnolle–Huber, "extracted from Theorem 14.2 from Lattimore and Szepesvári [2020]"):
  $\Pr_{\mathbb P}(E)+\Pr_{\mathbb Q}(E^c)\geq\frac12\exp(-KL(\mathbb P,\mathbb Q))$.
- **Theorem 11** (chain rule, following "Lemma 15.1 in Lattimore and Szepesvári [2020]"). "Consider two
  instances $I_P,I_Q$ that share the same arm set $\mathcal A$, online phase horizon $T$, offline sample size
  $\{T_S(a)\}_{a\in\mathcal A}$, but have two different reward distributions $P=(P^{(on)},P^{(off)})$,
  $Q=(Q^{(on)},Q^{(off)})$. For any non-anticipatory policy $\pi$, it holds that
  $KL(\rho_{P,\pi},\rho_{Q,\pi})=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\mathbb E_{P,\pi}[N_T(a)]\cdot KL(P^{(on)}_a,Q^{(on)}_a)+\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}T_S(a)\cdot KL(P^{(off)}_a,Q^{(off)}_a)$."
  Proof with densities ((47)–(49)); the offline sizes are deterministic, so they enter as $T_S(a)$, not
  as an expectation.

### Appendix D.2.1, proof of Theorem 3 (pp. 55–57), summary

- Claim (50), for a fixed $a$ with $\Delta(a)>0$:
  $\mathbb E_{P,\pi}[N_T(a)]\geq\frac{2(1-p)}{(1+\epsilon)^2}\cdot\frac{\log T}{\Delta(a)^2}+\frac{2}{(1+\epsilon)^2\Delta(a)^2}\cdot\log\left(\frac{\epsilon\Delta(a)}{8C}\right)-T_S(a)\cdot\max\left\{\left(1-\frac{\omega(a)}{(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)}\right),0\right\}^2$
  (as printed; the source calls Theorem 3 "the Lemma" there). Theorem 3 follows by multiplying by $\Delta(a)$ and summing.
- **Alternative instance (51).** Gaussian $Q$ with "$Q^{(\mathrm{off})}_k=P^{(\mathrm{off})}_k$ for all $k\in\mathcal A\setminus\{a\}$",
  $Q^{(\mathrm{on})}_a=\mathcal N(\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)+(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a),1)$ and
  $Q^{(\mathrm{off})}_a=\mathcal N(\mu^{(\mathrm{off})}(a),1)$ if $\mu^{(\mathrm{off})}(a)\geq\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)+(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)-V(a)$,
  $\mathcal N(\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)+(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)-V(a),1)$ otherwise. That is, the online mean of $a$ is raised
  by $(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)$ (so $a$ becomes the unique optimal arm with gap at least $\epsilon\Delta(a)$ to the
  others), and the offline mean of $a$ is raised just enough to be within $V(a)$ of the new online mean.
  The online laws of the other arms are not mentioned explicitly in (51); the KL computation uses only arm $a$,
  so they are kept. $Q\in\mathcal I_V$ is checked in both cases.
- **Steps (52)–(55).** Event $E=\{N_T(a)\geq T/2\}$; consistency on both instances gives
  $2CT^p\geq\mathbb E[\mathrm{Reg}_T(\pi,P)]+\mathbb E[\mathrm{Reg}_T(\pi,Q)]\geq\frac T2\epsilon\Delta(a)[\Pr_{P,\pi}(E)+\Pr_{Q,\pi}(E^c)]$
  $\geq\frac T4\epsilon\Delta(a)\exp[-\mathbb E_{\pi,P}[N_T(a)]KL(P^{(on)}_a,Q^{(on)}_a)-T_S(a)KL(P^{(off)}_a,Q^{(off)}_a)]$
  (Theorem 11 and Bretagnolle–Huber), with $KL(P^{(on)}_a,Q^{(on)}_a)=\frac{(1+\epsilon)^2\Delta(a)^2}{2}$ and
  $KL(P^{(off)}_a,Q^{(off)}_a)=\frac{\max\{(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)-[V(a)+(\mu^{(off)}(a)-\mu^{(on)}(a))],0\}^2}{2}$; taking logs
  gives $(1-p)\log T+\log\frac{\epsilon\Delta(a)}{8C}$ on the right, and rearranging gives (50).
  $\epsilon$ enters only through the size of the perturbation $(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)$ and the gap $\epsilon\Delta(a)$
  in the event bound; $\kappa_\epsilon$ is the $\log\frac{\epsilon\Delta(a)}{8C}$ term, $\mathit{Sav}_\epsilon$ the offline KL term
  divided by the online KL.

## Techniques

Change of measure on one arm (online and offline law moved together, the offline mean projected onto
the bias constraint), chain rule with a deterministic offline term, Bretagnolle–Huber (credited to
Lattimore–Szepesvári 2020, Theorem 14.2; chain rule after their Lemma 15.1). Upper bound: the minimum of
an online UCB and a pooled UCB with a bias correction $\frac{T_S}{N+T_S}V$.

Remark (not in the source): with $\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}(a)+(1+\epsilon)\Delta(a)\to\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}_*$ as $\epsilon\to0$, the
offline KL of the alternative is $\frac12\max\{\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}_*-V(a)-\mu^{(\mathrm{off})}(a),0\}^2$, i.e. the Gaussian
cost of moving the offline mean up to $\mu^{(\mathrm{on})}_*-V(a)$.
