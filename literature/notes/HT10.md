## Results

Statements copied from the text layer of the PDF; formulas rebuilt in LaTeX with the paper's symbols.

- **Definition (§2).** "Finally we define an index for $F\in\mathcal F$ and $\mu\in\mathbb R$:
  $D_{\inf}(F,\mu;\mathcal F)\equiv\inf_{G\in\mathcal F:\mathrm E(G)>\mu}D(F\|G)$."
- **Model (§3).** "We consider $\mathcal A=\{G:\mathrm{supp}(G)\subset[0,1]\}$ as a model $\mathcal F$ for the
  rest of this paper. When we adopt the model $\mathcal A$, it is convenient to use
  $D_{\min}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)\equiv\inf_{G\in\mathcal A:\mathrm E(G)\ge\mu}D(F\|G)$ instead of
  $D_{\inf}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)=\inf_{G\in\mathcal A:\mathrm E(G)>\mu}D(F\|G)$."
- **Lemma 2 (§3).** "$D_{\min}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)=D_{\inf}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)$ holds for all $F\in\mathcal A$ and
  $\mu<0$." Proof as printed: "$D_{\min}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)\le D_{\inf}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)\le D_{\min}(F,\mu+\epsilon;\mathcal A)$
  holds for arbitrary $\epsilon>0$ from the definitions of $D_{\min}$ and $D_{\inf}$.
  $D_{\min}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)=D_{\inf}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)$ follows by letting $\epsilon\downarrow0$, since we will
  prove in Theorem 7 that $D_{\min}(F,\mu;\mathcal A)$ is continuous in $\mu<0$."
  *Remark (not in the source):* the range "$\mu<0$" is printed in Lemma 2, in its proof and at the start of
  §4.1, while Theorems 7 and 8 have "$\mu<1$"; it looks like a remnant of the convention of the authors'
  preprint arXiv:0905.2776 (supports in $[-1,0]$). The printed proof gives the lemma for every $\mu<1$:
  Theorem 7(i) gives the continuity of $D'_{\min}$ in $\mu<1$, and Theorem 8 transfers it to $D_{\min}$
  (the §4.3 lead-in says so: "the differentiability and the continuity in Theorem 6 and 7 also hold for
  $D_{\min}$"). The page image was not viewed.
- **(4)–(5), §4.1 "A Dual Problem".** "For $\mu<0$, define $H(\nu;F,\mu)\equiv\mathrm E_F[\log(1-(X-\mu)\nu)]$"
  (with its first two derivatives in $\nu$, (4)) "and
  $D'_{\min}(F,\mu)\equiv\max_{0\le\nu\le\frac1{1-\mu}}H(\nu;F,\mu)$. (5)
  $D'_{\min}$ corresponds to the Lagrangian dual problem for $D_{\min}$."
- **Theorem 5.** The maximiser $\nu^*$ of (5): $D'_{\min}(F,\mu)=0$ if $\mu\le\mathrm E(F)$;
  $\nu^*=(1-\mu)^{-1}$ if $\mathrm E(F)\le\mu$ and $\mathrm E_F[(1-\mu)/(1-X)]\le1$; otherwise $H'(\nu^*)=0$ and (6).
- **Theorem 6.** "$D'_{\min}(F,\mu)$ is differentiable in $\mu\in(\mathrm E(F),1)$ for any $F\in\mathcal A$" with
  derivative $\nu^*$.
- **Theorem 7.** "$D'_{\min}(F,\mu)$ is continuous in (i) $\mu<1$ and (ii) $F\in\mathcal A$."
- **Theorem 8 (§4.3).** "$D_{\min}(F,\mu)=D'_{\min}(F,\mu)$ holds for all $F\in\mathcal A$ and $\mu<1$."
- Lemma 11: monotonicity in $\mu$; Lemma 12: $\nu^*\ge\frac{\mu-\mathrm E(F)}{\mu(1-\mu)}$; Lemma 14:
  $\sup_G D_{\min}(G,\mu)\le-\log(1-\mu)$ (statements read, proofs not checked).

## Numbering in later papers

HT15 cites the equality of the primal and the dual as "Honda and Takemura, 2010, Theorem 5" (its
Proposition 1), with the COLT 2010 version in its reference list. In the COLT PDF that result is
Theorem 8; Theorem 5 is the description of the maximiser. HT15's number matches the preprint
arXiv:0905.2776v3 (the MED paper, Machine Learning 2011), whose Theorem 5(i) is the dual formula for
supports in $[-1,0]$. arXiv:2111.09724 cites "Theorem 8 in [Honda and Takemura 2010]", matching the
COLT version. Cite this work as [HT10, Theorem 8] (COLT numbering).

## Techniques

The primal problem $D_{\min}$ over distributions on $[0,1]$ with mean at least $\mu$, and its
Lagrangian dual (5), a one-dimensional concave maximisation; the equality (Theorem 8) is proved
first for finite supports by Lagrange multipliers (Lemmas 9–10), then by approximation. The
optimal tilt $G^*$ of the proof of Theorem 8 has density $1/(1-(x-\mu)\nu^*)$ with respect to $F$
plus, in the boundary case, an atom at the endpoint.
