## Results

Statements copied from the page images; the source writes $\mathbb P$ and $\mathbb E$ in blackboard bold
("IP", "IE" in the text layer). $X\sim\mathsf{subG}(\sigma^2)$: $X$ is sub-Gaussian with variance proxy
$\sigma^2$ (Definition 1.2, not read in full).

- **Lemma 1.3 (p. 17).** "Let $X\sim\mathsf{subG}(\sigma^2)$. Then for any $t>0$, it holds
  $\mathbb P[X>t]\le\exp\big(-\frac{t^2}{2\sigma^2}\big)$, and $\mathbb P[X<-t]\le\exp\big(-\frac{t^2}{2\sigma^2}\big)$. (1.3)"
  Proof by the Chernoff bound (Markov's inequality applied to $e^{sX}$, then optimisation in $s>0$).
- **Lemma 1.4 (p. 18).** "Let $X$ be a random variable such that
  $\mathbb P[|X|>t]\le2\exp\big(-\frac{t^2}{2\sigma^2}\big)$, then for any positive integer $k\ge1$,
  $\mathbb E[|X|^k]\le(2\sigma^2)^{k/2}k\Gamma(k/2)$. In particular, $(\mathbb E[|X|^k])^{1/k}\le\sigma e^{1/e}\sqrt k$, $k\ge2$.
  and $\mathbb E[|X|]\le\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}$."
  Proof (pp. 18–19): $\mathbb E[|X|^k]=\int_0^\infty\mathbb P(|X|>t^{1/k})\mathrm dt\le2\int_0^\infty e^{-\frac{t^{2/k}}{2\sigma^2}}\mathrm dt$,
  then the substitution $u=\frac{t^{2/k}}{2\sigma^2}$ gives $(2\sigma^2)^{k/2}k\Gamma(k/2)$; the second statement from
  $\Gamma(k/2)\le(k/2)^{k/2}$ and $k^{1/k}\le e^{1/e}$; for $k=1$, $\sqrt2\,\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt{2\pi}$.
  *Reader's remark (not in the source):* the statement is for positive integers $k$; the first
  computation of the proof does not use that $k$ is an integer, so it gives the same bound for every
  real $k>0$.
- **Lemma 1.5 (p. 19).** "If (1.3) holds and $\mathbb E[X]=0$, then for any $s>0$, it holds
  $\mathbb E[\exp(sX)]\le e^{4\sigma^2s^2}$." The text after it: "we will sometimes write
  $X\sim\mathsf{subG}(\sigma^2)$ when it satisfies (1.3) and $\mathbb E[X]=0$."

## Techniques

- Moments from tails: $\mathbb E|X|^k=\int_0^\infty\mathbb P(|X|^k>t)\,\mathrm dt$ and a Gamma integral (proof of Lemma 1.4).
- Chernoff bound (proof of Lemma 1.3); Taylor expansion of the moment generating function with the
  moment bounds of Lemma 1.4 (proof of Lemma 1.5).
