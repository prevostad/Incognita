## Results

- **Abstract (positions 861–939 of the OpenAlex index; one word, "used", is absent from the index
  and was inferred; LaTeX rebuilt from tokens).** "The lower bound for the asymptotic minimax risk
  for $N\to\infty$ obtained by Vogel in [5] may be raised by a factor $2^{1/2}$. This is proved by
  applying the same method that was used in [5] to the optimal symmetric strategy for
  $\alpha+\beta=1$ that was discussed in [4]. Combining this lower bound with the upper bound given in
  [5] we find that the asymptotic minimax risk must be between $0.265N^{1/2}$ and $0.376N^{1/2}$."
  Setting (search snippet of the abstract): $N$ trials on two Bernoulli experiments with unknown
  success probabilities $\alpha$, $\beta$. Reference [5] is presumably Vogel (Vog60); not checked.
- Search snippet of the abstract: the algebra for minimax-risk strategies "seems to remain
  prohibitive already for $N$ as small as 5".
